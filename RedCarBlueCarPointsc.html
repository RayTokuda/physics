1D Motion Calculus AP Physics Quick Reference

Position $x(t)$

Gives location on 1D axis at time $t$. Polynomial: $x(t) = at - bt^2 + ct^3$.

Velocity $v(t) = x'(t)$

Derivative of position. Turning points occur when $v(t) = 0$ and velocity changes sign.

Acceleration $a(t) = v'(t)$

Rate of change of velocity: $a(t) = x''(t) = -2b + 6ct$.

Displacement vs Distance

Displacement: $\Delta x = x_f - x_i$. Distance: Sum of absolute movement lengths between turns.
